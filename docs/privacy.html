Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

SuperTV (“the app”) is a media player that plays streams from a subscription service that you configure. We do not collect any personal data. SuperTV has no servers of its own, does not create accounts, and never sends your information to the developer or to any third party.

Information you enter

To work, the app needs the credentials of your own subscription (server address, username, and password):

  • They are stored only on your device, securely, in the iOS/tvOS Keychain.
  • They are sent exclusively to the server you specify, to authenticate you and play your streams.
  • They are never transmitted to the SuperTV developer or to any third party.

Local cache

The app stores a local copy (cache) of your categories and channels on your device to work offline and load faster. This data never leaves your device, and you can clear it from Settings → Clear cache.

Network

The app connects only to the server whose credentials you enter. There is no analytics, tracking, advertising, or any connection to the developer’s services.

Content

SuperTV does not provide, host, or distribute any content. All content comes from the service you subscribe to and configure, under your own responsibility.

Deleting your data

When you log out or uninstall the app, the stored credentials and cache are removed from your device. Because we have no servers, there is nothing to delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
